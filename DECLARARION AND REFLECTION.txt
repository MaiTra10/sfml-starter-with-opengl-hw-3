b. ACTUAL USE vs. INTENT:
Describe how you actually used the tool(s). Did your usage align

I first started the assignment by prompting it to read through the assignment overview and explain
the concepts in a simple way and asked it to use an analogy, as I always do. The analogy it gave me was that of
assembling your own toy robot arm. The main takeaway was how you go piece by piece, and with the first 2 pieces you get
one point of rotation, and then when you add the second that second point can pivot on its own, but if you move the 
original it also moves the second, and so on. Since this assignment had a total of 5 moving parts and swivel points, this concept
helped me break up the problem into individual pieces and tackle them one at a time. And also making sure that the entire unit moved
as one.

And then when it came time to development I used AI to help me with debugging. I had a long running issue with rotation and the behaviour
of it because it was difficult to map out the rotation of so many axes in code. For example if I pressed R/F to move the elbow up or down, it would
instead spin around the origin instead of the joint. AI was really good at identifying this and correcting the matrix math to fix this. Beyond this specific
example I had similar struggles with rotation along the joint for almost every one and AI aided in similar ways.
